About the Group

One city. One name. Four businesses.

Perla Batumi Group is a privately held company on the Georgian Black Sea coast, operating in football, esports, hospitality and apparel.

Our story

Perla Batumi was founded in 2019 in Batumi and has grown into a group of four divisions with 289 operating staff. Football, esports, hospitality and apparel are run as separate businesses under one brand.

Our name

Perla is the word for pearl in a number of languages, and a pearl is made slowly, under pressure, by the sea. Batumi is the port city where we were founded and where we remain. The name reflects where we come from and what we build.

Our approach

Each division operates independently, with its own management, under shared standards of quality and a common identity. The Group is rooted in Batumi and invests in the city where it was founded.
